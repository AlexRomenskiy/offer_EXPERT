Before you visit

Fair questions.

It doesn't have to be two of the same. A common plan: parents right-size from the family house into a 2 bedroom, and the kids start with a 1 bedroom or a 1 bedroom + den. The sales team can walk each household through its own numbers, and your mortgage advisor can confirm what applies to you.

Separate homes, separate doors, separate keys. You decide how close: the same building, the same floor or a short walk apart, depending on what's available when you buy.

Ask the Experience Centre for the current construction timeline. Choosing early usually means more plans, floors and views to pick from, and time to sell the family house without rushing.

Less than you'd think. Royal Bay next door has schools, parks and shops, Langford has the major retailers, and Colwood adds three full-service supermarkets and over 50 restaurants and cafes. Most days, the gym, sauna, coworking lounge, rooftop and beach are already part of home.

No. Origin mixes 1 and 2 bedroom homes with 16 three-bedroom townhomes, plus a kids' play area in the courtyard and a coworking lounge for people who work from home. That mix is exactly why families can buy here together.

Strata fees depend on the size of the home. Ask the sales team for the exact figure on each home you're considering, so both households can plan with real numbers.
